Reported or verified?

Two labels, one rule: no fact without its source

Every university page uses the same two labels, so you always know how much weight a line can carry.

ReportedNot yet verified

Reported in public sources

What listings, registers and the university's own pages seen in search results say: the institution type, city, programme areas and website address. We show these as leads to check, with where we saw them and when. No fee, requirement, date or operating status is ever shown this way.

Verified

Verified on an official source

A fact we have read on the university's official website or in a Ukrainian government register, kept with its source address and the date we checked it, and reviewed before each intake. Only verified facts appear as fees, requirements, deadlines or programme details.
