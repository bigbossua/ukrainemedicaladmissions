What to check before you apply

  1. Find the official website from an official source. Look the university up in a Ukrainian government register rather than trusting an advert, a social-media page or an agency's copy of its site; if you found a website through a search, check its address against that register. How to recognise official channels.
  2. Confirm that your programme is open to international applicants for your intake, and the language it is taught in. If the website is unclear, ask the university's international office in writing.
  3. Get the tuition fee from the university, with its academic year. Ask for the payment schedule and every other mandatory charge, for example registration, accommodation or insurance. How to read a fee schedule.
  4. Compare the entry requirements with your qualifications: the school-leaving subjects and grades, any entrance test or interview, and the language level. How entry requirements are compared.
  5. Check recognition with your home regulator before you pay. Whether you can practise after graduating is decided by the regulator for your profession (medicine, dentistry, pharmacy or nursing) in the country where you plan to work, not by the university or by us.
  6. Look for the university's own statement for this academic year on whether, how and from which city it is teaching: in person, at a distance or both.
  7. Pay and send documents only through the university's own procedure. Never send money or original documents to anyone who cannot show you the university's written instructions.
