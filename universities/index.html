Which is the best medical university in Ukraine?

There is no official ranking of Ukrainian medical universities for international applicants, and we do not publish one. Compare institutions on facts you can verify on each profile: whether your programme is open to applicants from your country this intake, the language of instruction, the qualification title awarded, the published tuition fee with its academic year and payment schedule, other mandatory charges, and the entry requirements and documents. Where a fact has not yet been confirmed from an official source, the profile says so instead of guessing.
