    Step 5 of 5 Review and send

    Check your answers above. When you are ready, send them to us.

    Sending creates your candidate profile with us. It is not an application to a university, and nothing is sent to any university.

    What happens next

    1. We read your profile and reply by email with answers to your questions and the next steps we suggest. Where a university's requirements have not yet been verified from its official pages, we tell you so plainly.
    2. Nothing is sent to a university unless you confirm it, and then only through the university's own procedure.

    The four stages

    Enquiry → Draft application → Submitted to our service → Official submission to the university. This form is stage 1. The later stages are not available online yet. Read about the four stages.
