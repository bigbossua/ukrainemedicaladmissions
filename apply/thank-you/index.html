Thank you, we have your details

Your candidate profile or enquiry has reached Ukraine Medical Admissions. We will reply by email.

What this means

Your details have been received by Ukraine Medical Admissions only. They have not been sent to any university, and no application has been made on your behalf. No documents were uploaded.

What happens next

  1. We read what you sent us.
  2. We reply by email with answers to your questions and the next steps we suggest. Where a university's requirements have not yet been verified from its official pages, we say so plainly.
  3. An application goes to a university only after you confirm it, and only through the university's own procedure.

Please do not email scans of your passport or certificates. We do not need documents at this stage.

While you wait, you can read how to check a university's official website and how to read a fee schedule, or see the universities we are researching.

If you did not send this, email info@ukrainemedicaladmissions.com.

Ukraine Medical Admissions is an independent service. We are not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any university or government body. Admission decisions are made solely by the university.
