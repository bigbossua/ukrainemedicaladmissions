How to complain

Email info@ukrainemedicaladmissions.com with "Complaint" in the subject line, your name, the application or enquiry reference if you have one, what went wrong, and what you would like us to do.

What happens next

  1. We acknowledge your complaint within 3 working days.
  2. We investigate and reply with our findings and any remedy within 15 working days; if we need longer we tell you why and when to expect our answer.
  3. If you are not satisfied, you can ask for the complaint to be reviewed by the business owner, who replies within a further 10 working days.

Refunds and remedies

Where a paid service was not delivered as described, the remedy follows the refund terms shown when you chose that service. University fees are paid to the university and are subject to the university's own rules.

What we cannot change

We cannot overturn a university's admission decision or an authority's visa decision; complaints about those decisions must be directed to the body that made them.
