Medicine is the most sought-after programme among international applicants to Ukraine. Programmes are typically six years and lead to a medical degree whose exact title is set by each university. Below you will find only the universities whose medicine programme details we have recorded, each marked verified or pending.

Searching for “MBBS in Ukraine”?

Applicants from India, Pakistan and the Gulf often search for “MBBS in Ukraine”. Ukrainian universities do not award a degree called MBBS: each university sets its own qualification title, which we show on its profile once it has been verified from the official programme page. Whether a Ukrainian medical degree is recognised for practice in your country is decided by your home regulator, not by the university or by us — check that regulator’s current rules before you apply.

What to check before you apply

  • The exact qualification title awarded and its listing in the World Directory of Medical Schools.
  • Whether the English-taught stream is open to applicants from your country for the coming intake.
  • How clinical rotations are organised and where.
  • Licensing requirements in the country where you intend to practise — these are set by that country, not the university.
