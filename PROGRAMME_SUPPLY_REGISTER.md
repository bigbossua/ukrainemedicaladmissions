# PROGRAMME_SUPPLY_REGISTER.md — what Ukrainian universities actually offer in medicine and health sciences (research register, 2026-10-11)

**Status of everything in this file: snippet-level leads, not verified facts.** Official `.ua` websites cannot be opened from this environment (network policy, decision D5), so every entry comes from a search-result snippet whose URL and wording are recorded. A programme is **publishable** only after the verification procedure in `UNIVERSITY_DATA_REGISTER.md` §3 has been run against the official page. Nothing here may be copied onto the website.

Status key: **LEAD-OFFICIAL** (snippet from the university's own domain) · **LEAD-THIRD-PARTY** (state admissions portal `vstup.osvita.ua`, Ministry portal, aggregator or news — weaker) · **NO SNIPPET** (nothing found; not evidence of absence) · **VERIFIED** (none yet) · **OUT OF DATE** (snippet carries a past year).

## 1. Terminology that must be respected on the site

- Ukrainian higher education awards for medicine, dentistry and pharmacy are **integrated Master's degrees** ("Master of Medicine", "Master's degree", single-cycle, 300–360 ECTS) under national specialty codes **222 Medicine, 221 Dentistry, 226 Pharmacy, industrial pharmacy**; several 2025–2026 official documents also use new letter codes (**I2 Medicine, I1 Dentistry, I8 Pharmacy, I5 Nursing, I9 Public Health, A7 Physical culture and sport, D3 Management of health care**) — both systems appear in snippets. "MBBS" is a search term, never the award.
- **Nursing** is specialty **223 Медсестринство / Nursing**; snippets show *bachelor* programmes ("Бакалавр медсестринства", "Bachelor of Nursing") and, at some universities, a *master's* in nursing and *professional junior bachelor* (college-level) programmes. **No snippet anywhere uses the award names "BSN" or "MSN"**; the site must not use them except as explained search terms.
- **Public health** is specialty **229 / I9 Громадське здоров'я**; snippets show *master's* programmes (120 ECTS, 1 year 10 months), some new in 2025–2026 and some marked "not accredited" or "draft". **No snippet uses "MPH" as an award name.**
- **Physical therapy** is specialty **227 Фізична терапія, ерготерапія** (now also seen as "Терапія та реабілітація" with a Physical Therapy specialisation); snippets show bachelor (240 ECTS, 3 years 10 months) and some master's programmes — all in Ukrainian in the snippets found. This is a *clinical rehabilitation* qualification, distinct from **017 / A7 Physical culture and sport** (physical education / sport science), of which almost nothing medical-university-based was found.
- **Preparatory departments** for foreign citizens exist at most medical universities: typically a 10-month / one-academic-year course of Ukrainian (or, where stated, English for English-medium entrants) plus biology, chemistry, physics and sometimes mathematics/computer science, ending in a certificate; snippets describe entry to the first year as a *possibility* for graduates who pass the exams — **never a guarantee**.

## 4. Regulatory and terminology notes from the topic sweeps (snippet-backed; sources in `research/universities/2026-10-11-snippet-sweep-2.json`)

- **Specialty recoding (Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 1021 of 30.08.2024):** 229 Public Health → **I9**; 227 Physical therapy, ergotherapy → renamed **«Терапія та реабілітація»** (bachelor standard MES order No. 1541 of 29.10.2024; letter code unconfirmed, possibly I7); 017 Physical culture and sport → **A7** (field A, Education). Universities use old and new codes side by side in 2024–2026 documents.
- **Public health standards:** master level MES order No. 1383 of 12.12.2018; bachelor level MES order No. 1000 of 04.08.2020; a new I9 master standard was published as a **draft** in June 2026. Award wording: «бакалавр громадського здоров'я»; master's «диплом магістра». **No official university snippet uses "MPH" as its award title** (ASPHER's directory uses "Master of Public Health" as a listing label for Ternopil, Kharkiv and Ostroh — Ukrainian-taught, cost "NA").
- **Physical therapy profession:** World Physiotherapy lists the Ukrainian Association of Physical Therapy as a member since 2011 and records «Фізичний терапевт» as a protected title (data as at 30 June 2024); master's graduates sit the Unified State Qualification Exam; **no snippet describes a Ministry of Health register, licensing route or foreign-graduate recognition** — nothing may be said about practising abroad.
- **Preparatory routes:** terminology varies (preparatory department / faculty / International Faculty No. 1 / pre-study language course); the completion document is a "state certificate" or "certificate of completion", and **what it confers differs by university** — Ternopil's "entitles to continue their studies", Kharkiv's "gives the right to [enter higher education]"; Bukovinian's is the only English-track preparatory course found. The site must quote each university's own wording and never generalise.
- **Official route for foreigners:** applicants create an account in the **Unified Interdepartmental Information System**, may submit up to 20 applications, pay an application fee (3,100 UAH quoted, undated), and receive an **electronic invitation valid for six months** (one page says 180 days) used for the visa; universities cite MES Order No. 1541 of 01.11.2013 (admission of foreigners) and No. 504 of 05.05.2015 (recognition of foreign education documents — "nostrification"). The exact subdomain `apply.studyinukraine.gov.ua` did not appear in any snippet; `studyinukraine.gov.ua/invitation-for-study/` did.
- **Minimum school qualification:** portal pages require "complete secondary, secondary special or higher education" for bachelor entry plus language sufficiency judged by the admissions committee; **no official statement about applicants missing biology/chemistry/physics was found** — the readiness service must rely on each programme's verified requirement objects.
