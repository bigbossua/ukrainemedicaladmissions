What we do

Ukraine Medical Admissions helps international students understand their options at Ukrainian medical universities and prepare complete, accurate applications. We maintain a directory of universities and programmes in which every fact — fee, deadline, language, requirement — is tied to an official source and a check date.

How we verify information

  1. We record each fact from the university's official website, fee schedule or admissions document, or from a Ukrainian government register, and store the source URL.
  2. Each fact is marked verified only once it has been checked against that source; otherwise it is shown as pending or to be confirmed.
  3. We date every check and review profiles before each intake. Outdated facts are flagged rather than silently left in place.
  4. We do not publish rankings, student numbers, success rates or testimonials we cannot evidence.

Our independence

Ukraine Medical Admissions is an independent service. We are not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any university or government body. Admission decisions are made solely by the university. University fees are paid to the university. Any fee for our own services is explained in full — inclusions, exclusions and refund terms — before you choose it.
