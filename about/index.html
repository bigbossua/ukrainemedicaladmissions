What we do

Ukraine Medical Admissions helps international students understand their options at Ukrainian medical universities. Today you can send us a short candidate profile, and we reply by email with answers to your questions and the next steps we suggest. Help with preparing a complete, accurate application is planned as later stages open. See what we do today and what is planned.

We are also building a directory of universities and programmes. Each verified fact (fee, deadline, language, requirement) will show its official source and the date we checked it, and anything not yet checked is clearly marked. See the universities we are researching.

How we verify information

  1. We record each fact from the university's official website, fee schedule or admissions document, or from a Ukrainian government register, and keep the source address with it.
  2. A fact is marked verified only once it has been checked against that source. Until then it is shown as pending or to be confirmed.
  3. Every profile we publish shows the date of each check and is reviewed before each intake. Outdated facts are flagged rather than silently left in place.
  4. We do not publish rankings, student numbers, success rates or testimonials we cannot evidence.

Our independence

How we are paid, and our policy on referrals, advertising and rankings, are set out in our independence and referral disclosure. University fees are paid to the university. Any fee for our own services is explained in full (inclusions, exclusions and refund terms) before you choose it.
