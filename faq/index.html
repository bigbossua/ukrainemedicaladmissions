Applying

Is there a fee to start an application?

No. Starting an application on this site is free and does not commit you to anything. Any fee for our own support services is shown separately on your application dashboard before you choose it.

Does starting an application mean my application has been sent to a university?

No. An application is sent to a university only through that university's authorised procedure and only after you confirm. We clearly distinguish an enquiry, a draft, an application submitted to our service, and an official submission to a university.

Can you guarantee my admission?

No. Admission decisions are made solely by the university. We help you prepare a complete, accurate application; we do not and cannot guarantee the outcome, nor visas, scholarships or recognition of a qualification.

Are you part of a university or the Ukrainian government?

No. Ukraine Medical Admissions is an independent service and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any university or government body.

Our information

Where do your fees and requirements come from?

Only from the university's official website, published fee schedules and admissions documents, or Ukrainian government sources. Each fact shows its status (verified or pending) and the date we last checked it.

Why are some facts marked “to be confirmed”?

Because we have not yet verified them from an official source. We would rather show a gap than an invented or outdated figure. If you need that fact now, start an application and we will confirm it with you.

Are fees in different currencies or years comparable?

Not directly. We always show the academic year and currency, and we never merge different years or programme types into one figure.

Do you use real photos of each university?

On a university profile we use images from the university's official sources where we are allowed to. Generic stock photography is never presented as a specific campus, and every image is credited with its source and licence.

Still unsure? See the admissions guide, the fees guide, or contact us.
