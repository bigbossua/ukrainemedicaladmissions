What we do for applicants

Independent help for international students applying to medical universities in Ukraine. Below: what we do for you today, what we never promise, and what it costs.

  1. Home
  2. Services

What we do today (free)

1

Answers by email

Tell us your course, preferred universities and background in a short online profile. We read it and reply by email with answers to your questions and the next steps we suggest. Where a university's requirements have not yet been verified from its official pages, we say so.

Start your profile →
2

Document questions

The Apply Online form lets you mark each document as have, need or not sure. We answer document questions by email, including on translations, notarisation and legalisation.

Document guidance →
3

Choosing a university and course

We help you work out what to compare: whether your course is open to applicants from your country this intake, the language of instruction, the qualification title, the published fee and the entry requirements. Each fact appears on a university's profile once it has been verified.

See the universities we are researching →
4

What comes next

We explain by email what comes next at each stage, including the invitation letter and visa steps that the university and the authorities control.

The four stages →

What we never promise

Admission, a visa, recognition of a qualification in any country, scholarships or employment. Admission decisions are made solely by the university, visa decisions by the relevant authorities, and recognition by the regulator in the country where you plan to work. See our independence and referral disclosure.

What it costs

No paid service is offered at present, and starting an application is free. If a paid service is introduced, every option will be described in full before you choose it: inclusions, exclusions, price, when payment is due and the refund terms. Any fee of ours will always be shown separately from university fees, which are paid to the university.

Questions first? See the FAQ or contact us.
