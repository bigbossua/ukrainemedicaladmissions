How it works

  1. Start online — tell us your course, preferred universities and background. Free, non-binding, nothing is sent to a university.
  2. We confirm the current requirements for your choices from the universities' official pages and send you a document checklist.
  3. You prepare and upload your documents through the secure application dashboard when it is available; we review and advise.
  4. You confirm — only then is an application submitted through the university's authorised procedure.

What we charge

Starting an application is free. Where we offer paid support services, every option is described in full — inclusions, exclusions, price, when payment is due and the refund terms — before you choose it, and always separately from university fees, which are paid to the university. Paid options are shown only after your application has been received and reviewed.

What we never promise

Admission, a visa, recognition of a qualification in any country, scholarships or employment. Admission decisions are made solely by the university; visa decisions by the relevant authorities. Ukraine Medical Admissions is an independent service. We are not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any university or government body. Admission decisions are made solely by the university.
