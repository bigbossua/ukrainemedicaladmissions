How fees are published

Each university sets its own tuition fee per programme and academic year, usually in US dollars or euros, payable per year or per semester. Fees can change between intakes. For that reason every fee on this site carries the academic year it applies to and the date it was last verified. Fees from different years or currencies are never combined into a single figure.

MBBS fees in Ukraine

Many applicants search for “MBBS fees in Ukraine”. Ukrainian universities do not award an MBBS; the equivalent programme is Medicine, usually six years, and each university publishes its own fee for it. The table below lists the medicine fee per university as soon as it has been verified from the official schedule.

Cost of medical education in Ukraine: what a total includes

  • University charges — e.g. application or registration fees, invitation-letter processing, medical insurance arranged by the university. Listed per university where published.
  • Accommodation — university hostels or private rental; prices shown only where a current official source exists.
  • Living costs, visa fees, travel, document translation and legalisation — vary by person and country; we do not publish estimates we cannot source.
  • Our service fee — charged by Ukraine Medical Admissions, not the university, and shown separately on the application dashboard once you have applied. No service fee is charged to start an application.

Any “total cost” we show in future will list its components, sources and dates. An estimate is never a guaranteed total.

Fees for Indian students

We are often asked whether Ukrainian universities charge Indian students a different fee. We will state that only from each university's official fee schedule: if a schedule prices by nationality, the profile will say so with the currency, academic year, payment schedule and source. Recognition of a Ukrainian medical degree in India is governed by the National Medical Commission's current regulations for foreign medical graduates — check the NMC's own published rules before you apply; we do not advise on them.
